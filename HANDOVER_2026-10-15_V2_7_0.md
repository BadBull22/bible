# Handover — 2026-10-15 — v2.7.0: the sermon builder, rebuilt

Desktop (Windows) 2.7.0. The sermon builder was compared with Logos, Sermonary, Proclaim,
SermonCentral and the free tools, and rebuilt in four phases. The rule throughout: **no AI**.
The app finds, arranges and prompts; the preacher writes every word.

Backup taken before the work started: `V:\Code\bible-backups\2026-10-15_before-sermon-builder-2`
(repo bundle, resources, `userdata.db`, `settings.json`) and the git tag
`backup/before-sermon-builder-2` (pushed).

## 1. What is new

**Gather** (`src/sermon.ts`, `src/components/SermonBuilder.tsx`, `PassageStudy.tsx`)
- A passage (`John 3`, `Romans 8:28-39`) gathers the passage, then:
  - the passage section by section
  - book and chapter introductions
  - people and places
  - cross-references per section
  - every commentary per section
  - the Hebrew and Greek words it repeats
  - Simeon's outlines
  - Spurgeon's sermons on it
  - illustrations and quotations
  - the preacher's notes
  - **In your Library**: places in the downloaded books, devotionals, dictionaries and commentaries that cite the passage.
- A topic does the same by subject. **In your Library** for a topic is a real search of the downloaded books and devotionals:
  - word forms are matched ("faith" also finds "faithful")
  - a few results come from each book
  - each result shows the paragraph that says the most.
- **Study this passage** opens everything on one passage in one panel.
- The summary bar jumps to each group; long groups show 15 with "Show all"; each group has "Tick all".
- Material used in an earlier sermon is marked "used before".

**Draft** (`src/sermonTemplates.ts`)
- Big idea and purpose fields.
- 13 templates: points, expository, big idea (Robinson), Me-We-God-You-We (Stanley), Hook-Book-Look-Took (Richards), problem/answer, Four Pages (Wilson), narrative, gospel, funeral, wedding, communion, free.
- The preacher can save their own templates (stored in `userdata.db`, table `sermon_templates`).
- *Build the draft* gives each point Scripture → Explain → Illustrate → Apply → Transition, with a guiding question, and places the ticked material under it.
- Formatting: bold, italic, bullets, `[[blanks]]`.
- Manuscript or outline view, with side navigation.
- Words written and minutes against the target length.

**Document, handouts, slides** (`src/sermonOutputs.ts`, `src-tauri/src/slides.rs`)
- Document: A4 or A5, large print, blank lines on or off.
- Listener outline; fill-in-the-blanks sheet with its answer key; small-group guide.
- PowerPoint slides: a hand-written `.pptx`, saved to `Documents\Bible Concordance`. It opens in PowerPoint (checked by exporting the slides to PNG through COM).

**Preach mode** (`PreachMode.tsx`)
- Full screen; scroll the whole sermon or one section at a time.
- Clock against the target length; text size; light or dark.
- Sections with nothing in them are left out.

**My sermons and plan** (`SermonPlanner.tsx`, `LectionaryView.tsx`, `src/lectionary.ts`)
- Each sermon has a stage (idea → researching → drafting → ready → preached) and a place it will be preached. These are new columns `stage` and `venue` on `sermons`; older databases are migrated.
- Views: by series, or on a calendar. The calendar names each Sunday from the lectionary.
- *Plan a series* lays a book out a passage at a time, on dated sermons.
- **Lectionary**: the 1979 Book of Common Prayer's Sunday and holy-day lectionary, Years A/B/C, with the collect of each day.
  - `src/lectionary.ts` computes the church year: Easter, Advent 1, Epiphany Sundays, Lent, Propers by "Sunday closest to", and fixed and movable holy days. It was checked against known dates for 2026–27.
  - *Prepare it now* saves a sermon for the day and gathers its readings.

**The verse panel while reading** (`CrossRefGraph.tsx`, `LibraryMentions.tsx`)
- Under the cross-references are two new lists: "Spurgeon preached on this" and "In your Library".
- A Library item opens the book at that paragraph, the dictionary at its entry, or the commentary at its passage.

**Fixed on the way:** `BookPanel` ignored a "go to this section" request when React drew it twice (development mode) or when a second book was opened quickly. The stale load is now ignored.

## 2. New data (all public domain) and how it is rebuilt

| File | From | Script |
|---|---|---|
| `src-tauri/resources/simeon_outlines.json` (1.1 MB, 2,051 outlines) | Simeon, *Horae Homileticae* (1832–33), Internet Archive OCR of 21 vols | `data-pipeline/build_simeon.py <folder of vol01..21.txt>` |
| `src-tauri/resources/illustrations.json` (0.7 MB, 619) | Moody's *Anecdotes* and *Stories*, Spurgeon's *Gleanings among the Sheaves* (Gutenberg 19830, 33024, 42657) | `data-pipeline/build_illustrations.py <folder>` |
| `src-tauri/resources/spurgeon_sermons.json` (2.9 MB, 3,527 sermons, 3,466 texts) | CCEL ThML, `https://ccel.org/ccel/s/spurgeon/sermonsNN.xml`, NN = 01..63 | `data-pipeline/build_spurgeon.py <folder of v01..v63.xml>` |
| `src/lectionary.json` (148 KB, 232 days) | The Lectionary Page's 1979 BCP pages (citations and collects only) | `data-pipeline/build_lectionary.py <folder>` |

Notes on the data:
- **Simeon:** about 44 lines out of 14,000 still carry an OCR fragment. References that don't exist in the KJV are dropped.
- **Spurgeon:** CCEL's heading for No. 683 reads "6823"; the script corrects it (`MISPRINTS`). Volume 52 orders its `div1` attributes differently, and the parser handles both. `opening` is the sermon's first paragraph; `url` is its CCEL page (internet needed to read it).
- **The lectionary site** (lectionarypage.net) refuses requests without a browser User-Agent. The pages come in two layouts, and the script handles both. Only citations, names and collects are kept, never the NRSV text on the pages.

## 3. The Revised Common Lectionary — not included, on purpose

The CCT's permissions page lets congregations reproduce the RCL for their own worship. Any other "print or electronic resource" needs written permission from the copyright administrator, Augsburg Fortress (copyright@augsburgfortress.org). An app given away to the public is the second kind.

The 1979 Book of Common Prayer is not under copyright, so its lectionary is used instead. Note that Wikisource's copy of the BCP is the 2007 printing, which prints the *RCL*; don't take the lectionary from there.

If the owner gets written permission, the RCL can be added as a second table: the same JSON shape, with a choice in the Lectionary view. Its credit line is "Revised Common Lectionary, Copyright © 1992 Consultation on Common Texts. Used by permission."

## 4. The Library reference index

`library.db` has two new tables:
- `lib_refs(module, tbl, row_id, pos, book, chapter, verse, verse_end)` turns the books' ⟦Book|ch|v|v_end|label⟧ markers round.
- `lib_refs_done(module, installed_at)` records which modules are indexed.

The index is built lazily by `library_mentions` (`ensure_ref_index`) the first time it's needed after a module is installed:
- On the owner's library (Owen, *Daily Light*, ISBE, Webster, four commentaries; about 138,000 references) the first build took 6.5 s; later lookups take about 20 ms.
- Removing a module deletes its rows.
- Reinstalling changes `installed_at`, which re-indexes the module.

## 8. Redesign after the owner's first test (2026-10-16), still 2.7.0

The owner found the builder "not very user friendly", wanted the found groups to stay in view, wanted pictures, music, video and files, and wanted items to land in the right sections. So the builder was redone before release. A backup of the earlier state is in `V:\Code\bible-backups\2026-10-16_before-builder-layout`.

- **One Prepare screen in two panels** (`SermonBuilder.tsx`, `SermonFind.tsx`, `SermonOutline.tsx`). Each panel scrolls on its own: what was found on the left, the sermon on the right.
  - The group chips and a filter stay at the top of the left panel.
  - "Read" (the chapter) and "Study this passage" open inside the left panel.
  - The book list is hidden while the builder is open (`App.tsx`, `builder-pane`).
  - The tabs are Prepare · Print & handouts · Slides · My sermons. Series, place, stage and length sit behind "More details".
- **Placement** (`src/sermonLayout.ts`): `partsOf` and `slotsOf` read the flat item list as parts (points, sections) and slots. `placeMaterial` and `placeFor` put an added item into the section whose role takes it:
  - A clicked section always wins.
  - A clicked point means its fitting section.
  - Otherwise the item goes to the first empty fitting section, then the least-filled one.
  - `movePart` drags a whole point; `moveIntoSlot` drags one item into another section.
  - Checked by a 20-assertion script, run with Node 24's built-in TypeScript support (`node file.mts`, importing `src/sermonLayout.ts`, which has type-only imports).
- **Point cards**: empty sections fold into "+ Explain" chips, and an empty level-1 section is one line with "Write…". Each card has one "Add ▾" menu.
- **Saving**: work is kept in `localStorage` (`sermon:work`) as it changes, so it survives a restart. After the first Save, the sermon saves itself 2.5 s after each change. Undo (Ctrl+Z) covers structural changes.
- **Media** (`src-tauri/src/sermon_media.rs`, `src/sermonMedia.ts`, `MediaPicker.tsx`):
  - Added files are copied into `<app data>\sermon-media\` under a content hash, which `valid_name` checks.
  - Links are kept in the sermon.
  - The nature photos are the Android zip (`backgrounds-v1`, SHA-256 checked), downloaded once into `<app data>\backgrounds\`.
  - Downloaded Bible pictures can be used too.
  - Media shows in the document (pictures as PNG data URLs via canvas; links as `SheetRun.link`), in Preach (pictures, clickable links), and on slides.
- **Slides** (`SlidesEditor.tsx`, `slides.rs`):
  - Slides are made from the sermon until edited (`SermonBody.slides`), with a background photo for the set (`slideBackground`).
  - Slide kinds: title, point, scripture, text, song, image, link.
  - The PPTX can now carry pictures (`ppt/media`), a cropped and darkened photo background, and hyperlinks (web, or `file:///` for kept files). The theme's link colour is gold.
  - Checked by exporting from PowerPoint through COM.
  - Videos are links, not embedded (the owner's choice is still open).
- Tests: `cargo test --lib` 63 passed (new: `slides_carry_pictures_backgrounds_and_links`, `files_are_kept_once_under_safe_names`).
- **Open:** Present mode (a second-screen presenter view) is waiting for the owner's answer.
