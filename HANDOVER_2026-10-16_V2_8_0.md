## 2. What changed

### Task 7 — slides
- **Scripture slides** are cut at whole verses: at most four verses and about 280 characters a
  slide; a single long verse is split at a sentence. Each slide names its own verses
  ("John 3:16–17 · BSB"). `src/scriptureSlides.ts` (`versesOf`, `scriptureSlides`,
  `tidyQuotes`), re-exported from `sermonOutputs.ts`; it has no imports so Node can test it.
  Only verse numbers that run in order from the passage's first verse count, so "the 12
  tribes" stays inside its verse.
- **Verse numbers on/off** (`work.slideVerseNumbers`, default on). For the preacher's own
  edited slides, turning it off strips the numbers from their Scripture text.
- **The previews shrink to fit** (`useFit` in `SlidesEditor.tsx`, a ResizeObserver).
- **The bar is sticky**, with a visible button per slide kind ("+ Words", "+ Song", …).
- **Per slide:** "This slide's background: Same as all · Plain · A photo…" and "Use for all
  slides"; "Picture beside the words" for heading, words, song, Scripture and title slides.
  `SlideSpec.background` is now per slide (absent = the one for all, `""` = plain navy);
  `SlideSpec.picture` is new. `slides.rs`: `Slide.picture`, the words take the left 55% and
  the picture the rest; a Rust test covers it.
- **The space inside BSB quotation marks** ("“ Rabbi", "again. ”") is closed up on slides and in
  Preach (`tidyQuotes`).
- Not done, still the owner's choice: a Present mode with a presenter view, and embedding
  videos in the PowerPoint.

### Task 6 — Review
- A **Review** tab between Prepare and Print & handouts (`SermonReview.tsx`). It is built from
  the sermon itself (not from `SheetBlock`s with a `src`, as the task suggested): the same
  order and rules as `sermonBlocks`, with the preacher's parts as in-place text boxes (title,
  big idea, purpose, points, section names and text, notes) that write through the same
  `edit` / `editLabel` / `set` as Prepare. Gathered material shows as printed; it can be
  removed (Undo) and a non-Scripture quotation can be **shortened** — the shortened text is the
  sermon's own copy in `material.text`, the original kept in `material.original` (new optional
  field) and restorable.
- Print & handouts says "Something to change? Edit it in Review".

### Task 5 — Hebrew and Greek words
- `SermonWords.tsx`, shown in place of the left list (like Read and Study): a passage's words
  from BSB or KJV (whichever carries Strong's numbers), first twelve verses; click a word for a
  card (original word, transliteration, pronunciation, Strong's number and definitions, how
  often used, any downloaded lexicon's entry) and **Add to the sermon** (a `word` material,
  key `w:<strongs>`, the same shape the gatherer makes, so it goes to Explain).
- Opened by **Words** under a Scripture item on the left and in the sermon on the right, and
  by a **Look up a Hebrew or Greek word** box at the top of the left side (English gloss, a
  Strong's number, or the word in Greek or Hebrew letters). A transliteration ("agape") is not
  matched: `original_word_lookup` has no transliteration search.

### Task 4 — when it was preached, and finding sermons
- **Tables** (in `userdata.db`, `SCHEMA`): `sermon_preachings(id, sermon_id, date, venue,
  audience, notes, length_min)`, `sermon_refs(sermon_id, book, chapter, verse, verse_end)`, and
  `sermon_fts` (FTS5, porter: title, series, topics, big idea + purpose, the preacher's words).
  `sermon_save` re-indexes (`index_sermon`); `sermon_delete` removes all three.
- **Migration** (`first_index`, `PRAGMA user_version` 0 → 1): every sermon indexed, and one
  preaching added for each sermon already "preached" with a date.
- **Recording a preaching** makes the sermon "preached" with its latest date
  (`follow_preachings`); the open sermon in the builder follows (`onChanged` in
  `SermonBuilder`). Choosing the stage Preached opens the form. UI: `SermonPreachings.tsx` in
  the sermon's details.
- **"Used before"** now says when and where ("— preached 8 Mar 2026 South Point (3 times)"):
  `SermonUsage.preached`.
- **Find a sermon** (My sermons): `SermonSearch.tsx` → `sermon_search` with words, a passage
  (parsed with `parseRef`; "Romans 8" = the chapter, "Romans 8:28" = overlap), date range
  (any preaching, or the planned date), series, place (sermon or any preaching), stage, and
  sort (newest, oldest, most preached, title).
- **Backup**: `sermon_preachings` (by sermon title and series), restored once; restored sermons
  are re-indexed.
- Rust tests: `preachings_search_and_the_backup`, `sermons_from_before_the_tracker_get_their_preaching`.

## 5. The owner's decision on e-Sword and MySword libraries (2026-10-16)

- **No downloads by the app** from eswordlibrary.com, biblesupport.com, e-Sword or MySword:
  their modules are community uploads with no per-file licence, so the app cannot confirm
  the right to copy them, and a disclaimer would not change that.
- **The app may search them, but the user downloads.** In the Library, a "More modules
  (community libraries)" part: a search box that opens each site's own search for the words
  in the browser, plus plain links to the sites, with one line saying they are community
  libraries and to check each module's rights. Nothing is fetched or copied by the app.
- The user then adds the downloaded files with the e-Sword/MySword import (task 1), which
  shows "Only add modules you have the right to use…".
- Public-domain modules from these sites may be used by the developer as test files for the
  importer.
