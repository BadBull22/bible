========================================================================
THE 100 MOST ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT THE BIBLE AND CHRISTIANITY
Complete Reference Document for Papers, Reviews, and Study
========================================================================


CATEGORY: THEOLOGY: GOD, JESUS, AND THE HOLY SPIRIT
--------------------------------------------------
Question 1: Is Jesus God?
Answer: Yes, Jesus is God incarnate. Scripture explicitly calls Him God, attributes divine characteristics to Him, and shows Him receiving worship.
Scripture Reference(s): John 1:1, John 20:28, Colossians 2:9, Philippians 2:6

Question 2: How can there be one God, yet three Persons (the Trinity)?
Answer: The Trinity is the doctrine that God exists as one divine essence or substance, but in three distinct, co-eternal, and co-equal persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 28:19, 2 Corinthians 13:14, Peter 1:2

Question 3: Who is the Holy Spirit?
Answer: The Holy Spirit is the third Person of the Trinity, a personal being who convicts the world of sin, guides believers into truth, and empowers them for godly living.
Scripture Reference(s): John 14:16-17, Acts 5:3-4, Romans 8:26

Question 4: If God is good, why does He allow suffering and evil?
Answer: God created a perfect world, but human rebellion (sin) brought brokenness into creation. God allows free will and uses suffering to build character, draw people to Himself, and ultimately defeat evil.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 3:17-19, Romans 8:28, Genesis 50:20, Revelation 21:4

Question 5: Did God create Satan, and how did Satan fall?
Answer: God created Satan as a beautiful, high-ranking angel named Lucifer. Satan rebelled against God due to pride and was cast out of heaven along with a third of the angels.
Scripture Reference(s): Isaiah 14:12-15, Ezekiel 28:12-17, Luke 10:18

Question 6: Is God responsible for people's sins?
Answer: No, God is perfectly holy and cannot sin, nor does He tempt anyone to sin. Sin originates from human desires and choices.
Scripture Reference(s): James 1:13-14, 1 John 1:5, Habakkuk 1:13

Question 7: Does God know everything? (Omniscience)
Answer: Yes, God has complete, infinite knowledge of all things past, present, future, and even all possibilities.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 John 3:20, Isaiah 46:9-10, Psalm 147:5

Question 8: Can our prayers cause God to change His mind?
Answer: God is unchanging (immutable) in His character and ultimate decrees, but Scripture shows that He responds dynamically to prayer, incorporating human prayers into His sovereign plans.
Scripture Reference(s): Malachi 3:6, James 5:16, Exodus 32:14

Question 9: Why does it sometimes feel like God is silent?
Answer: God's silence is not absence. He uses seasons of silence to test faith, build perseverance, teach reliance on His written Word, and accomplish a deeper spiritual purpose.
Scripture Reference(s): Psalm 13:1-2, Psalm 22:1-2, James 1:2-4

Question 10: Do Christians and Muslims worship the same God?
Answer: No. While both faiths claim Abrahamic roots, Christianity worships a Triune God who has a unique Son, Jesus Christ, who died and rose again—doctrines that Islam categorically rejects.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 John 2:23, John 14:6, John 5:23

Question 11: If God knew Adam and Eve would sin, why did He create them?
Answer: God chose to create humanity out of love, valuing genuine relationship which requires free will. He did so knowing that He would ultimately display His ultimate grace through the sacrifice of Jesus.
Scripture Reference(s): Ephesians 1:4, Revelation 13:8, Romans 5:8

Question 12: Is God a male or a genderless spirit?
Answer: God is a spirit and does not possess a physical body or physical gender, though He reveals Himself primarily using masculine titles (Father, King) to signify His authority and relationship to us.
Scripture Reference(s): John 4:24, Luke 24:39, Matthew 6:9

Question 13: What does it mean that Jesus is the Son of God?
Answer: The title 'Son of God' does not denote biological offspring, but rather identity, status, and equality with God the Father, expressing His divine nature and unique relationship.
Scripture Reference(s): John 5:18, Hebrews 1:3, Matthew 16:16

Question 14: Did Jesus really rise from the dead?
Answer: Yes, the bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ is a historical reality and the core foundation of the Christian faith, verified by numerous eyewitness accounts.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 15:3-8, Luke 24:39, Matthew 28:5-6

Question 15: Is blasphemy against the Holy Spirit the unforgivable sin?
Answer: The unforgivable sin is the continuous, willful rejection of the Holy Spirit's witness to Jesus Christ, resulting in a hardened heart that persistently refuses to repent.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 12:31-32, Mark 3:28-30, John 16:8


CATEGORY: SCRIPTURE & BIBLE AUTHENTICITY
--------------------------------------------------
Question 16: Is the Bible completely true and without error?
Answer: Evangelical Christianity asserts that the original manuscripts of Scripture are inerrant, meaning they are entirely truthful and free from error in all that they affirm.
Scripture Reference(s): 2 Timothy 3:16, 2 Peter 1:20-21, Psalm 119:160

Question 17: How was the Bible put together (the Canon)?
Answer: The books of the Bible were recognized over time by the early church based on apostolic authorship or endorsement, consistency with known doctrine, and widespread acceptance as divinely inspired.
Scripture Reference(s): Luke 1:1-4, 2 Peter 3:15-16, Ephesians 2:20

Question 18: Why is there an Old and a New Testament?
Answer: The Old Testament records God's covenant with Israel and points forward to a coming Messiah. The New Testament records the fulfillment of that promise through Jesus and the New Covenant.
Scripture Reference(s): Hebrews 8:6, Matthew 5:17, Luke 24:44

Question 19: What are the Apocrypha, and why are they excluded from Protestant Bibles?
Answer: The Apocrypha are Jewish historical texts written between the testaments. They are excluded from Protestant Bibles because they were never quoted by Jesus, never part of the Hebrew canon, and contain doctrinal contradictions.
Scripture Reference(s): Romans 3:2, Luke 24:44

Question 20: Which Bible translation is the best?
Answer: There is no single 'best' translation. Word-for-word (formal equivalence) translations like the ESV or NASB are great for study, while thought-for-thought (dynamic equivalence) like the NIV are highly readable.
Scripture Reference(s): Nehemiah 8:8, 1 Corinthians 14:9

Question 21: How should a beginner start reading the Bible?
Answer: Beginners are usually encouraged to start in the New Testament with the Gospel of John to understand who Jesus is, followed by Romans, and then the Old Testament book of Genesis.
Scripture Reference(s): John 20:31, Romans 10:17, 1 Peter 2:2

Question 22: Are Christians still required to follow Old Testament laws?
Answer: Christians are under the New Covenant. The civil and ceremonial laws of Israel were fulfilled in Christ, but the moral laws (like the Ten Commandments) reflect God's eternal character and remain binding.
Scripture Reference(s): Romans 6:14, Galatians 3:24-25, Matthew 5:17

Question 23: Should Christians keep the Saturday Sabbath?
Answer: The Sabbath was a sign of the Mosaic covenant. The New Testament church moved its primary day of worship to Sunday ('The Lord's Day') to celebrate Christ's resurrection, and warns against legalism regarding days.
Scripture Reference(s): Colossians 2:16-17, Acts 20:7, Romans 14:5

Question 24: Is the Bible historically accurate?
Answer: Yes, historical, geographical, and archaeological discoveries continue to confirm the reliability, timelines, and people mentioned across biblical narratives.
Scripture Reference(s): Luke 3:1-2, Psalm 33:4, Proverbs 30:5

Question 25: What does it mean that the Bible is 'living and active'?
Answer: It means the Holy Spirit uses the written words of scripture to supernaturally penetrate hearts, expose motives, and transform lives across all generations.
Scripture Reference(s): Hebrews 4:12, Isaiah 55:11, 1 Peter 1:23


CATEGORY: SALVATION, GRACE, AND FAITH
--------------------------------------------------
Question 26: What must I do to be saved?
Answer: Salvation requires repenting of your sins and placing your personal faith and trust in the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ as the full payment for your sin.
Scripture Reference(s): Acts 16:31, Romans 10:9, Ephesians 2:8-9

Question 27: Can a Christian lose their salvation?
Answer: True believers are sealed by the Holy Spirit and kept securely by God's power. Those who completely abandon the faith permanently usually demonstrate they were never truly saved.
Scripture Reference(s): John 10:28-29, Romans 8:38-39, 1 John 2:19, Ephesians 1:13

Question 28: Is Jesus the only way to heaven?
Answer: Yes, Jesus explicitly claimed to be the exclusive way to God, and the apostles preached that salvation can be found in no other name.
Scripture Reference(s): John 14:6, Acts 4:12, 1 Timothy 2:5

Question 29: What is sin?
Answer: Sin is any violation of God's moral law, whether by missing the mark of perfection, rebelling against His boundaries, or failing to do the good we know we should do.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 John 3:4, Romans 3:23, James 4:17

Question 30: What is the difference between justification and sanctification?
Answer: Justification is a one-time legal declaration where God declares a believer righteous based on Christ. Sanctification is the lifelong process of becoming holy and Christlike.
Scripture Reference(s): Romans 5:1, 1 Thessalonians 4:3, Philippians 1:6

Question 31: Is water baptism required for salvation?
Answer: No, salvation is by grace alone through faith alone. Baptism is an outward, public symbol of an inward spiritual reality and an act of obedience, not a requirement for eternal life.
Scripture Reference(s): Ephesians 2:8-9, Luke 23:42-43, 1 Corinthians 1:17

Question 32: What does predestination mean?
Answer: Predestination refers to God's sovereign choice before creation to elect and determine beforehand those who would be brought into a saving relationship with Himself.
Scripture Reference(s): Ephesians 1:4-5, Romans 8:29-30, John 6:44

Question 33: How can I forgive myself for past sins?
Answer: Forgiving yourself means fully accepting that God has already washed away your guilt through Christ. If God does not condemn you, you do not need to condemn yourself.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 John 1:9, Romans 8:1, Psalm 103:12

Question 34: Can a deathbed conversion really save someone?
Answer: Yes. God's grace is not based on human effort or time served. Anyone who sincerely repents and trusts Christ at the very end of their life is fully saved.
Scripture Reference(s): Luke 23:42-43, Matthew 20:1-16

Question 35: Why do Christians still sin if they are a 'new creation'?
Answer: While a believer's spiritual position is renewed, they still live in human flesh and struggle against an old, fallen nature until they are glorified in heaven.
Scripture Reference(s): Galatians 5:17, Romans 7:15-25, 1 John 1:8


CATEGORY: DEATH, HEAVEN, HELL, AND THE AFTERLIFE
--------------------------------------------------
Question 36: What happens to a Christian immediately when they die?
Answer: The soul of a believer immediately goes to be consciously in the presence of the Lord, awaiting the final bodily resurrection at the end of the age.
Scripture Reference(s): 2 Corinthians 5:8, Philippians 1:23, Luke 23:43

Question 37: Is Hell a real place of literal fire and torment?
Answer: Yes, Jesus and the biblical writers explicitly describe Hell as a literal place of eternal conscious punishment, separation from God, and spiritual agony.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 25:41, Matthew 25:46, Mark 9:43, Revelation 20:15

Question 38: Will pets or animals go to heaven?
Answer: The Bible does not clearly state if our earthly pets have eternal souls, but it does show that the New Earth will be filled with animal life as part of redeemed creation.
Scripture Reference(s): Isaiah 11:6-9, Revelation 19:11

Question 39: Can someone who commits suicide go to heaven?
Answer: Suicide is a tragic sin, but it is not the unforgivable sin. A true Christian's salvation is secured by Christ's sacrifice, which covers all sins, past, present, and future.
Scripture Reference(s): Romans 8:38-39, John 10:28

Question 40: Will we recognize our loved ones in heaven?
Answer: Yes. Scripture indicates that our individual identities continue after death, and we will possess heightened recognition, relationships, and unity with others.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 13:12, Matthew 17:3, 2 Samuel 12:23

Question 41: Is Purgatory mentioned in the Bible?
Answer: No, the concept of Purgatory is not in Scripture. The Bible states that Christ's death fully paid for sin, and that to be away from the body is to be present with the Lord.
Scripture Reference(s): Hebrews 9:27, Hebrews 10:14, Romans 8:1

Question 42: Do humans become angels when they die?
Answer: No. Humans and angels are entirely different classifications of created beings. Believers will actually be raised higher than angels and will judge them.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 6:3, Hebrews 1:14, Psalm 8:4-5

Question 43: What will our resurrected bodies be like?
Answer: Believers will receive physical, immortal, glorious bodies like Jesus' resurrected body, completely free from sickness, decay, aging, and physical limitations.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 15:42-44, Philippians 3:21, 1 John 3:2

Question 44: What is the New Jerusalem?
Answer: The New Jerusalem is the magnificent heavenly city that descends from God to the New Earth, serving as the eternal dwelling place where God lives among redeemed humanity.
Scripture Reference(s): Revelation 21:1-4, Revelation 21:10-27

Question 45: Will there be marriage or families in heaven?
Answer: Jesus explained that human marriage ends at physical death. In heaven, there is no need for procreation, and our focus turns to a greater spiritual family dynamic centered on Christ.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 22:30, Mark 12:25


CATEGORY: PROPHECY AND THE END TIMES
--------------------------------------------------
Question 46: What are the primary signs of the End Times?
Answer: Scripture warns of an increase in wars, natural disasters, moral decay, widespread deception, false prophets, and the global preaching of the gospel before the end.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 24:4-14, 2 Timothy 3:1-5

Question 47: What is the Rapture?
Answer: The Rapture is the event where Jesus Christ returns in the clouds to instantly catch up living and deceased believers into the air to be with Him forever.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17, 1 Corinthians 15:51-52

Question 48: Who is the Antichrist?
Answer: The Antichrist is an end-times political leader empowered by Satan who will deceive the nations, blaspheme God, persecute believers, and demand global worship.
Scripture Reference(s): 2 Thessalonians 2:3-4, 1 John 2:18, Revelation 13:1-8

Question 49: What is the Mark of the Beast?
Answer: The Mark of the Beast is a physical indicator on the right hand or forehead required by the Antichrist's regime to engage in any form of commerce or economic activity.
Scripture Reference(s): Revelation 13:16-17, Revelation 14:9-11

Question 50: What is the Tribulation?
Answer: The Tribulation is a final seven-year period of unprecedented global distress, conflict, and divine judgment upon a rebellious world before Christ's physical return.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 24:21, Daniel 9:27, Revelation 6-19

Question 51: What is the Millennial Kingdom?
Answer: The Millennium is the literal 1,000-year reign of Jesus Christ on earth from Jerusalem, fulfilling God's promises of peace, justice, and restoration to Israel.
Scripture Reference(s): Revelation 20:1-6, Isaiah 9:6-7, Zechariah 14:9

Question 52: Can anyone predict the exact date of Christ's return?
Answer: No. Jesus explicitly stated that no man knows the day or the hour of His return, and anyone claiming to calculate a date is operating outside scripture.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 24:36, Mark 13:32, Acts 1:7

Question 53: What is the Battle of Armageddon?
Answer: Armageddon is the final world conflict where the nations assemble under the Antichrist to fight God, ending in total defeat by the physical return of Jesus Christ.
Scripture Reference(s): Revelation 16:16, Revelation 19:11-21, Zechariah 14:1-4

Question 54: What is the Great White Throne Judgment?
Answer: It is the final post-millennial judgment where all unbelievers throughout human history are evaluated by their works and sentenced to the Lake of Fire.
Scripture Reference(s): Revelation 20:11-15, John 5:28-29

Question 55: Will Christians go through the Tribulation?
Answer: Theological viewpoints differ. Pre-tribulationists believe Christ rescues the church before it starts; Mid/Post-tribulationists expect believers to endure parts or all of it.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Thessalonians 5:9, Revelation 3:10, Matthew 24:29-31


CATEGORY: MORALITY, ETHICS, AND DAILY LIVING
--------------------------------------------------
Question 56: What does the Bible say about homosexuality?
Answer: Scripture consistently defines marriage exclusively as a lifelong union between one man and one woman, characterizing homosexual acts as contrary to God's creative design.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 2:24, Leviticus 18:22, Romans 1:26-27, 1 Corinthians 6:9-10

Question 57: Is sex before marriage a sin?
Answer: Yes. The Bible strictly commands believers to avoid fornication or sexual immorality, reserving all forms of physical sexual intimacy exclusively for marriage.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 6:18, Hebrews 13:4, 1 Thessalonians 4:3

Question 58: When is divorce permitted for a Christian?
Answer: God desires marriage to be permanent. However, Scripture allows for divorce in specific cases involving unrepentant sexual unfaithfulness or abandonment by an unbelieving spouse.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 19:9, 1 Corinthians 7:15, Malachi 2:16

Question 59: Is masturbation a sin?
Answer: While not named directly, it almost always involves lustful thoughts outside of marriage, a lack of self-control, and using sexual desires self-indulgently, which scripture forbids.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 5:28, 1 Thessalonians 4:4-5, Colossians 3:5

Question 60: Are Christians allowed to get tattoos?
Answer: While Leviticus 19:28 forbade pagan cultic incisions, modern Christians view tattoos through Christian liberty, evaluating their motives, content, and whether it honors their body as God's temple.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 6:19-20, Romans 14:23, 1 Corinthians 10:31

Question 61: Is drinking alcohol a sin?
Answer: Drinking alcohol in moderation is not strictly forbidden, but Scripture explicitly condemns drunkenness, addiction, and acts that cause a fellow believer to stumble.
Scripture Reference(s): Ephesians 5:18, Proverbs 20:1, Romans 14:21, 1 Timothy 5:23

Question 62: Is gambling a sin?
Answer: The Bible does not explicitly name gambling, but it heavily warns against greed, a love of money, trying to get rich quick, and poor stewardship of financial resources.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Timothy 6:10, Hebrews 13:5, Proverbs 13:11, Matthew 6:24

Question 63: Are Christians required to tithe 10% of their income?
Answer: The 10% tithe was required under the Mosaic Law. New Testament believers are encouraged to give cheerfully, generously, and proportionately according to how God has blessed them.
Scripture Reference(s): 2 Corinthians 9:7, 1 Corinthians 16:2, Malachi 3:10

Question 64: Is viewing pornography a sin?
Answer: Yes. Pornography fuels lust, objectifies individuals, and violates Jesus' clear standard that looking at someone with lustful intent constitutes committing adultery in the heart.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 5:28, Job 31:1, 1 John 2:16

Question 65: Is using foul language a sin?
Answer: Yes. Christians are explicitly commanded to keep corrupt, filthy speech out of their mouths, using their words only to encourage, build up, and bring grace to listeners.
Scripture Reference(s): Ephesians 4:29, Colossians 3:8, Matthew 15:11


CATEGORY: SCIENCE, CREATION, AND HISTORY
--------------------------------------------------
Question 66: Does the Bible support evolution?
Answer: No. Genesis outlines a direct, purposeful creation by God where plants and animals reproduce 'according to their kinds,' and humans were uniquely made in the image of God.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 1:1, Genesis 1:26-27, Colossians 1:16

Question 67: How old is the Earth according to the Bible?
Answer: Young Earth creationists calculate an age of 6,000–10,000 years based on literal genealogies. Old Earth creationists allow for billions of years, interpreting Genesis 'days' metaphorically.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 1:1, Psalm 90:4, 2 Peter 3:8

Question 68: Were the days in Genesis literal 24-hour days?
Answer: Literal creationists note that the Hebrew word 'yom' combined with 'evening and morning' indicates standard 24-hour cycles. Day-Age theorists believe they denote long geological epochs.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 1:5, Exodus 20:11

Question 69: Does the Bible mention dinosaurs?
Answer: The word 'dinosaur' was coined in 1841, but Job describes massive creatures like 'Behemoth' and 'Leviathan' whose structural features closely resemble giant reptilian species.
Scripture Reference(s): Job 40:15-24, Job 41:1-34

Question 70: Where did Cain get his wife?
Answer: Cain married one of his sisters or nieces. In early human history, marrying close relatives was necessary to populate the earth and did not carry genetic risks before mutations accumulated.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 4:17, Genesis 5:4

Question 71: Was Noah's Flood global or local?
Answer: Scripture describes the flood covering 'all the high hills under the whole heaven,' suggesting a global catastrophe that wiped out all land-dwelling air-breathing life outside the Ark.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 7:19-23, 2 Peter 3:6

Question 72: Does the Bible oppose scientific exploration?
Answer: No. The Bible teaches that God created an orderly universe that reflects His glory, encouraging humanity to explore, study, and care for creation.
Scripture Reference(s): Psalm 111:2, Proverbs 25:2, Psalm 19:1

Question 73: Who were the Nephilim?
Answer: The Nephilim were ancient giants or terrifying warriors. Many interpreters believe they were the offspring of fallen angels ('sons of God') and human women.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 6:4, Numbers 13:33

Question 74: What was the Tower of Babel?
Answer: Babel was a unified human rebellion where people built a massive tower to make a name for themselves. God judged them by confusing their language and scattering them globally.
Scripture Reference(s): Genesis 11:1-9

Question 75: Does the Bible condemn interracial marriage?
Answer: No. God's boundaries for marriage are spiritual, forbidding believers from marrying unbelievers. All ethnic groups belong to one single human race descending from Adam.
Scripture Reference(s): Acts 17:26, 2 Corinthians 6:14, Galatians 3:28


CATEGORY: ANGELS, DEMONS, AND THE SUPERNATURAL
--------------------------------------------------
Question 76: Are guardian angels biblical?
Answer: Angels actively guard, protect, and serve believers, especially children, though Scripture doesn't definitively state whether every individual has one specific assigned angel.
Scripture Reference(s): Psalm 91:11, Matthew 18:10, Hebrews 1:14

Question 77: Can a true Christian be demon-possessed?
Answer: No. A true Christian is permanently inhabited and sealed by the Holy Spirit. Demonic forces can oppress, attack, and influence a believer externally, but they cannot possess them.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 John 4:4, 1 Corinthians 6:19, 2 Thessalonians 3:3

Question 78: What is spiritual warfare?
Answer: Spiritual warfare is the invisible conflict between God's kingdom and Satan's demonic forces, fought by believers using spiritual armor, prayer, and scriptural truth.
Scripture Reference(s): Ephesians 6:11-12, 2 Corinthians 10:3-5, 1 Peter 5:8

Question 79: Is speaking in tongues required for all Christians?
Answer: No. The Apostle Paul explicitly states that spiritual gifts are distributed individually by the Holy Spirit, and not every Christian receives the gift of tongues.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 12:11, 1 Corinthians 12:29-30, 1 Corinthians 14:19

Question 80: Does God still perform miracles and physical healings today?
Answer: Yes, God is all-powerful and sovereignly heals people and performs miracles today in response to prayer, though the specific office of a 'faith healer' may have ceased.
Scripture Reference(s): Hebrews 13:8, James 5:14-15, Psalm 103:3

Question 81: Should Christians avoid horoscopes, tarot cards, and psychics?
Answer: Yes. God strongly condemns all forms of occult involvement, witchcraft, sorcery, mediumship, and divination, as they seek supernatural guidance apart from God.
Scripture Reference(s): Deuteronomy 18:10-12, Galatians 5:19-21, Isaiah 47:13-14

Question 82: How can I discern God's will for my life?
Answer: Discerning God's will involves regular study of Scripture, consistent prayer, seeking wise godly counsel, evaluating circumstances, and maintaining a heart surrendered to Him.
Scripture Reference(s): Romans 12:2, Psalm 119:105, Proverbs 11:14, Proverbs 3:5-6

Question 83: What is 'laying out a fleece' to know God's will?
Answer: Derived from Gideon, it refers to asking God for a specific situational sign. While God tolerated Gideon's weak faith, Christians are called to walk by faith and biblical wisdom, not signs.
Scripture Reference(s): Judges 6:36-40, 2 Corinthians 5:7, Matthew 12:39

Question 84: Are there different ranks of angels and demons?
Answer: Yes, Scripture references different structural tiers, naming archangels, cherubim, seraphim, rulers, authorities, powers, and spiritual forces of darkness.
Scripture Reference(s): Ephesians 6:12, Colossians 1:16, Jude 1:9, Jude 6

Question 85: What is spiritual discernment?
Answer: Spiritual discernment is the Holy Spirit-given ability to distinguish truth from error, evaluate spiritual influences, and recognize what is pleasing to God.
Scripture Reference(s): Hebrews 5:14, 1 John 4:1, Philippians 1:9-10


CATEGORY: CHURCH DYNAMICS AND DENOMINATIONS
--------------------------------------------------
Question 86: Can women serve as pastors or elders?
Answer: Views differ. Complementarians believe Scripture limits church governance and teaching positions over men to qualified males. Egalitarians believe women can fill all ministry leadership roles.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Timothy 2:12, 1 Timothy 3:1-7, Galatians 3:28

Question 87: What are the main differences between Protestants and Catholics?
Answer: Protestants hold to salvation by faith alone through grace alone and Scripture as the sole infallible authority (Sola Scriptura). Catholics emphasize tradition, church authority, and sacramental cooperation.
Scripture Reference(s): Ephesians 2:8-9, Galatians 1:6-9, 2 Timothy 3:16

Question 88: Are Jehovah's Witnesses and Mormons Christian?
Answer: Theologically, no. Both groups deny central biblical doctrines, such as the full deity of Jesus Christ, the Trinity, and salvation by grace alone through faith alone.
Scripture Reference(s): Galatians 1:8, 2 John 1:9, Jude 1:3

Question 89: Why are there so many different Christian denominations?
Answer: Denominations exist because of secondary differences in church governance, baptism methods, end-times views, and worship preferences, while mostly agreeing on core biblical essentials.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 1:10, Ephesians 4:3-6, Romans 14:1-4

Question 90: Is church attendance mandatory for a Christian?
Answer: Attending church does not save you, but Scripture clearly commands believers not to forsake gathering together, as the local church is vital for accountability, community, and service.
Scripture Reference(s): Hebrews 10:24-25, 1 Corinthians 12:27, Acts 2:42

Question 91: What is transubstantiation?
Answer: Transubstantiation is the Roman Catholic belief that the bread and wine literally transform into the physical body and blood of Jesus during Communion. Protestants see it as a memorial symbol.
Scripture Reference(s): Luke 22:19, 1 Corinthians 11:24-26, Hebrews 10:12

Question 92: What is church discipline?
Answer: Church discipline is a formal biblical process of correcting an unrepentant believer to restore them to fellowship and preserve the purity of the local church body.
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 18:15-17, 1 Corinthians 5:1-5, Galatians 6:1

Question 93: What is the biblical definition of the church?
Answer: The church is not a physical building, but the global body of all true believers who have placed their faith in Jesus Christ, manifesting locally in specific congregations.
Scripture Reference(s): Colossians 1:18, 1 Corinthians 12:13, Ephesians 1:22-23

Question 94: What are the biblical ordinances for the local church?
Answer: Protestants recognize two structural ordinances established directly by Jesus: Water Baptism and the Lord's Supper (Communion).
Scripture Reference(s): Matthew 28:19, 1 Corinthians 11:23-26

Question 95: What is the role of a church deacon?
Answer: Deacons are qualified servants chosen to manage physical, administrative, and charitable needs within the church, allowing elders/pastors to focus on prayer and preaching.
Scripture Reference(s): Acts 6:1-6, 1 Timothy 3:8-13


CATEGORY: MIND, HEALTH, AND LIFESTYLE
--------------------------------------------------
Question 96: Can a Christian see a secular therapist or psychiatrist?
Answer: Yes. While biblical counseling is preferred for spiritual problems, medical professionals can help treat biological brain chemistry and mental health struggles. Believers should filter all advice through Scripture.
Scripture Reference(s): Protestants 11:14, 1 Thessalonians 5:21, Colossians 2:8

Question 97: How should a Christian handle anxiety and depression?
Answer: Believers should bring anxieties to God in prayer, fill their minds with scriptural truth, seek support within Christian community, and pursue medical treatment if there is an underlying chemical imbalance.
Scripture Reference(s): Philippians 4:6-7, 1 Peter 5:7, Psalm 42:11

Question 98: How can I discover my spiritual gifts?
Answer: Discovering spiritual gifts involves actively serving in a local church, noting where your efforts bear fruit, receiving confirmation from leadership, and assessing what ministries you are passionate about.
Scripture Reference(s): Romans 12:6-8, 1 Corinthians 12:4-7, 1 Peter 4:10

Question 99: What does it mean to take care of your body as a temple?
Answer: It means treating your physical body with respect, avoiding substance abuse, sexual immorality, and self-harm, recognizing that your physical self belongs to God and houses His Spirit.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Corinthians 6:19-20, Romans 12:1, 1 Corinthians 10:31

Question 100: How do I balance supporting family with helping the poor?
Answer: Scripture insists that providing for your immediate family is a primary spiritual obligation, but also commands believers to actively give out of their excess to help the vulnerable and poor.
Scripture Reference(s): 1 Timothy 5:8, James 1:27, Proverbs 19:17, 2 Corinthians 8:13-14
