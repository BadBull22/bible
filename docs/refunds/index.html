In short: Bible Concordance is free, so there is nothing to pay for and nothing to refund. It has no purchases, no subscriptions, no in-app payments and no adverts, and it never asks for money.

Free, and it stays free

The Windows app and the Android app can be downloaded, installed and used at no cost, with no account and no registration. No feature is locked behind a payment, and there is no “pro” version. The developer does not take payments and does not accept donations: if you would like to give something back, please give to your local church or a charity.

If you were charged

  • You were not charged by the developer. Anyone who asks you for money for Bible Concordance is not the developer, is not acting with the developer's permission, and is breaking the terms.
  • Please ask the seller, the app store or your payment provider (bank or card company) for a refund, and keep the receipt.
  • Please also tell us on GitHub, so the copy can be reported. The only genuine download links are on bible4me.org and the project's GitHub releases page.

Things you buy from other people

Some people add a Bible they have bought, such as a Bible from a publisher or an e-Sword or MySword module, or use their own paid key for a service such as api.bible or api.esv.org. Those purchases are between you and the publisher or the service, and any refund is theirs to give under their own policy. The developer receives none of that money.

If the app does not work for you

You can uninstall it at any time and you will have lost nothing. If something is broken, please tell us on GitHub.

Questions about any of this? Write to the developer on GitHub. Ask on GitHub →
