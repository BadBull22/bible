Windows

How to install it
  1. Press the Microsoft Store button. The Store opens on your computer.
  2. Press Get (or Install).
  3. When it finishes, open Bible Concordance from the Start menu.
What is in the free extras
The Bibles and study books that come with the app work straight away. Extra translations, commentaries, books and pictures are free optional downloads from inside the app, so the first install stays small.

Direct download (.apk)
On a computer?Point your phone's camera at this code to open the Android page on your phone, then download it there.
  • Android 7 or newer
  • about 90 MB
  • Updates itself
How to install the direct download
  1. Tap Direct download on your phone. If the browser warns that the file might be harmful, choose Download anyway: it says that about every app that does not come from a store.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open, or find the file in your Files app under Downloads and tap it.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
Samsung says the app is blocked or not valid?
Samsung phones are stricter than most about apps that do not come from a store. The app is the same file, and it installs once your phone is told to allow it.
  1. Open Settings, search for Install unknown apps and tap it.
  2. Tap the switch next to the app you downloaded the file with, such as Chrome, Samsung Internet or My Files.
  3. Go back and open the downloaded file again. If Play Protect warns about it, choose Install anyway (sometimes under "More details").
Still blocked? Newer Galaxy phones have Auto Blocker. Go to Settings, Security and privacy, Auto Blocker, switch it off, install the app, then switch it back on. Only do this for files you trust, such as this one: check its fingerprint below.
Updating, and moving your notes
Installing a newer version over an older one keeps everything: your bookmarks, highlights, notes and reading plans. The direct download looks once a day for a newer version and offers it (you can switch that off). If you later move to the Google Play version, save a backup first (in the app: More, Save a backup), because removing the old copy deletes the notes stored in it, then restore the backup in the new one.
Check the file is genuine
The file's SHA-256 fingerprint is 7fc87053a6cb350781a90b5c1c0a75612f614fb4ba0834b34e57a54b72070e50. After downloading, you can compare it with the one your computer calculates for the file (on Windows, in PowerShell: Get-FileHash BibleConcordance_Android_3.1.2.apk).

Questions and answers

Is it really free?
Yes. Bible Concordance is free to use and share. It will never be sold or charged for.
Do I need an account or the internet?
No account. No internet needed to read, search and study: the Bibles and study tools are on your device. You only need the internet to download optional extras.
Does it collect anything about me?
No. Your bookmarks, highlights, notes and prayers stay on your device. See the privacy page.
Can I use it on both my computer and my phone?
Yes. At home on your Wi-Fi the two keep your bookmarks, highlights and notes the same. Nothing goes through any cloud or account of ours.
Is there an iPhone or Mac version?
Not at the moment: Windows and Android only.
How do I update?
Windows updates through the Microsoft Store. Android updates through Google Play, or the direct-download version tells you when a new one is ready.
