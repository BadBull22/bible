Windows

The full study app for your computer.

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

How to install it
  1. Press the Microsoft Store button. The Store opens on your computer.
  2. Press Get (or Install).
  3. When it finishes, open Bible Concordance from the Start menu.
What is in the free extras
The Bibles and study books that come with the app work straight away. Extra translations, commentaries, books and pictures are free optional downloads from inside the app, so the first install stays small.

Android

A pocket companion for every day.

On a computer?Point your phone's camera at this code to open Bible Concordance in Google Play on your phone, then press Install.
  • Android 7 or newer
  • about 90 MB
  • Updates through Google Play
How to install it
  1. Press the Google Play button above on your phone (or scan the code with its camera). Google Play opens.
  2. Press Install.
  3. When it finishes, press Open, or find Bible Concordance among your apps.
Moving from the old direct-download version?
Bible Concordance used to be offered as a file to download from this website. It now comes only from Google Play, which keeps it up to date for you. If you still have the old file's version on your phone, save a backup first (in the app: More, Save a backup), because removing the old copy deletes the notes stored in it. Then install Bible Concordance from Google Play and restore the backup in it (More, Restore). The two apps cannot be installed over each other.
Keeping your phone and computer in step
At home on your Wi-Fi, the Android app and the Windows app can keep your bookmarks, highlights, notes and reading plans the same. Nothing goes through any cloud or account of ours.

Questions and answers

Is it really free?
Yes. Bible Concordance is free to use and share. It will never be sold or charged for.
Do I need an account or the internet?
No account. No internet needed to read, search and study: the Bibles and study tools are on your device. You only need the internet to download optional extras.
Does it collect anything about me?
No. Your bookmarks, highlights, notes and prayers stay on your device. See the privacy page.
Can I use it on both my computer and my phone?
Yes. At home on your Wi-Fi the two keep your bookmarks, highlights and notes the same. Nothing goes through any cloud or account of ours.
Is there an iPhone or Mac version?
Not at the moment: Windows and Android only.
How do I update?
Windows updates through the Microsoft Store. Android updates through Google Play.
